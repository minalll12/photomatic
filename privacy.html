— Privacy —

Kept on your device

Photomatic is a photo booth that lives entirely in your browser. Here is everything it does with your photos, in plain words.

01No accounts

There’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

02No uploads

Your photos are taken, edited and printed inside your browser. They are never sent to us or to anyone else: not the photos, not the finished strips, not the videos or story collages.

A file only leaves your device when you choose to download or share it yourself.

03Stored only in this browser

Strips you save to My Album are kept in this browser’s own storage on this device. Another browser or another device won’t see them, and we can’t see them either.

Photomatic also remembers two small settings here: whether sound and the screen flash are on.

04Clearing your album

Open My Album and choose Clear album to remove every strip, or open a single strip and choose Delete.

Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes the album and the settings too.

05The camera

Your browser asks before the camera is used. Photomatic uses it only while you’re in the booth, and switches it off as soon as you carry on to editing, leave the booth or close the page.

Nothing is recorded in the background, and you can always upload photos you already have instead.

Like any website, the host that serves these pages may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the site running. Photomatic adds no cookies, analytics or trackers.
